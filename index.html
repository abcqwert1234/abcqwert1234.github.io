Crowbar Ascent

Your hands are crowbars. Hook, haul and swing your way up the tower before you run out of energy.

  • Grip or trigger: hook the crowbar tip onto planks, pegs, gears and sliders
  • Pull your hands to haul yourself; let go mid-swing to fling upward
  • Energy drains while hanging, refills on ledges (checkpoints)
  • Left stick walks on ledges; A / X returns to your last ledge
